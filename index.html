Check Aadhaar, PAN, GST and company documents in seconds

Open the document and press Scan. The numbers are found, validated, cross-checked and — for digitally signed files — the signature is tested for tampering.

  • Aadhaar Verhoeff checksum, masked on screen, one-click permanent masking of the first 8 digits.
  • PAN structure and holder type; GSTIN check character, state code and embedded-PAN match.
  • Company certificates CIN decoded (state, year, ownership) and cross-checked with the date of incorporation.
  • Digital signatures Validate Signature, View Signed Version, Compare to Current Version and certificate viewer — like Acrobat.
These are offline checks. They catch typos, impossible numbers and edited files, but they cannot confirm that a number was really issued. Every result links to the official portal for that final step.

Questions, answered

Is it really free? Do I need an account?

Yes, it is free and there is no sign-up. The source code is open under the MIT Licence, so you can also host your own copy.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Documents are opened and edited in your browser's memory and never sent to a server. The only network requests are: loading this website, a one-time download of the OCR engine and language data when you first use OCR, and the official government websites you choose to open. Read the Privacy Policy for details.

Does it replace Adobe Acrobat?

It covers the everyday jobs — editing, OCR, merging, organizing, converting, signing, redacting and form filling — in a familiar layout. It does not do password protection/encryption, certificate-based digital signing, form design or long-term-validation signatures. Use desktop software for those.

Can I rely on the document verification?

Treat it as a fast first check, not proof. It validates number formats and checksums (Aadhaar, PAN, GSTIN, CIN…), cross-checks details against each other, and checks whether a digital signature was altered after signing. Whether a number was really issued must be confirmed on the official portal — each result links straight to it. See the Disclaimer.

Why does my digital signature say “unknown”?

A signature is only “valid” when its certificate chain leads to a trusted certificate. India's Root CA (CCA) and the licensed authorities under it are built in, so GST, PAN, e-Aadhaar and e-Mudhra signatures validate on their own. “Unknown” means the chain does not end at a trusted certificate — for example a self-signed signer or an authority outside India's PKI. Open the certificate's Trust tab and choose “Add to Trusted Identities” only after confirming its fingerprint with the issuer.

How do I install it on my desktop?

Click Install app on this page (Chrome or Edge), or use the install icon in the address bar. In Safari choose File → Add to Dock (macOS) or Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone/iPad). Once installed it opens in its own window, works offline and can open PDFs from your file manager.

Which fonts can I use for text?

The standard PDF fonts, Poppins (bundled, works offline) and 55+ popular Google Fonts that load the first time you pick them and are then kept on your device. Latin text is embedded as real, selectable text; Indian-language fonts and any characters a font doesn't contain are saved as an exact picture of the text. You can add any other Google Font by typing its name.

Which browsers work?

Recent versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari. Installing as an app works in Chrome, Edge and Safari 17+.

Is it safe to use with Aadhaar cards?

Files stay on your device and Aadhaar numbers are masked on screen by default. You can also burn a permanent black box over the first 8 digits before sharing. Share a masked copy whenever the full number is not required.
