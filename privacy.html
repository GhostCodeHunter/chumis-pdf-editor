Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what Chumi's PDF Editor (“the app”, “we”) does and does not do with information. In short: your documents are processed on your own device and are never uploaded to us.

Summary. No accounts. No analytics or advertising cookies. No server that receives your files. A few small items are stored in your browser so the app can work (listed below), and a few third-party services are contacted in the situations listed below.

1. Your documents

  • PDFs, images and other files you open are read and edited in your browser's memory. They are not transmitted to us or to any server operated for this app.
  • Edited files are created on your device and saved only when you click Download.
  • Closing a document tab or the browser tab releases it from memory. We have no copy and cannot recover it for you.

2. Sensitive identity documents (Aadhaar, PAN, GST, etc.)

The verification tool reads numbers from the document locally. Numbers are not sent anywhere, are not logged, and are not stored by the app. Aadhaar numbers are masked on screen by default and the exported report masks all but the last four digits. If you click an “official site” button, your browser opens the relevant government website in a new tab; nothing from your document is passed to it automatically — anything you type there is governed by that site's own policy.

2A. Information stored in your browser

Stored locally on your device using the browser's local storage / cache, and never sent to us:

ItemWhyHow to remove it
Saved signature imagesSo you can re-use a signature you drew, typed or uploaded.Delete the signature in the Fill & Sign panel, or clear this site's data in your browser.
Trusted certificates (public certificates you imported)So signature validation can recognise issuers you trust.Remove them in the Digital signatures panel, or clear site data.
Downloaded fonts and your recently used / added font namesSo fonts you picked load instantly and work offline afterwards.Clear this site's data in your browser settings.
Cached app files (service worker)So the app opens quickly and works offline once installed.Uninstall the app or clear site data.

The app does not use cookies. It does not use third-party analytics, advertising or fingerprinting scripts.

3. Third parties your browser may contact

WhenWhoWhat they can see
Loading the websiteThe host of the site (for example GitHub Pages)Normal web-server logs: your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. Your documents are not involved.
First use of OCRjsDelivr (CDN for the Tesseract.js engine) and the Tesseract language-data hostThe same standard request details. The engine runs on your device; your page images are not sent.
Choosing a Google Font for the first timejsDelivr, which mirrors the Google Fonts collection through the open-source Fontsource projectThe same standard request details (IP address, browser type) and the name of the font file requested. The fonts are then kept in your browser cache; your documents are not involved.
Clicking an “official site” linkThe relevant government website (UIDAI, Income Tax Department, GSTN, MCA, Udyam, etc.)Whatever you choose to enter on that site.

If the site owner has configured an optional live-verification service, it is only contacted when you press “Check live”, and the owner must describe it separately.

4. Children

The app is a general-purpose tool and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

5. Security

Because files never leave your device, the main protections are those of your own computer and browser. Keep your browser up to date, and avoid using shared or public computers for sensitive documents. Redaction in this app permanently flattens the affected page into a picture, but you should always re-open the saved file and check that the covered information is really gone before sharing it.

6. Your choices

You can clear everything the app stores by clearing this site's data in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the installed app. There is no account to close.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes, this page will be updated and the date above will change.

8. Contact

Questions or concerns can be raised through the project's repository (see the link in the page footer when it is available) or with the person who gave you this site's address.

Site operators: if you host your own copy and add analytics, accounts, uploads or other services, you must update this policy to describe them and meet the privacy laws that apply to you.
