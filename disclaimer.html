Please read this before relying on any result. Chumi's PDF Editor helps you inspect and edit documents. It is not an official verification service and is not connected to any government authority.

1. Government-document verification

  • Offline checks only. The tool checks number formats, published checksums (for example Aadhaar's Verhoeff check and the GSTIN check character), structural rules (PAN holder type, CIN fields) and consistency between values found in the same document.
  • A passing result does not prove a number or document is genuine. A number can have a valid format and checksum without ever having been issued. Only the issuing authority can confirm that — use the official-site button shown next to each result (UIDAI, Income Tax e-Filing, GST portal, MCA, Udyam).
  • A failing result can be a reading error. Text is read from the PDF or by OCR and may be wrong, especially on scans.
  • Not a KYC or compliance tool. Do not use it as the sole basis for onboarding, lending, tax or legal decisions. Entities that collect or store Aadhaar numbers must follow the Aadhaar Act, UIDAI rules and other applicable law.
  • No affiliation: Chumi's PDF Editor is not affiliated with UIDAI, the Income Tax Department, GSTN, the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, the Controller of Certifying Authorities or any other authority.

2. Digital-signature validation

  • The validator recomputes the signed fingerprint of the document and checks the signer's RSA signature. If either fails, the document or signature was changed.
  • It builds the certificate chain from the file. A signature is shown as valid only when the chain ends at a certificate that you have chosen to trust. Importing a certificate is your decision — confirm its thumbprint with the issuer first.
  • Not checked: certificate revocation (CRL/OCSP), trusted timestamps, long-term-validation data, and whether later changes were permitted by the signer.
  • Whether an electronic signature is legally valid depends on the law, the certifying authority and the circumstances. This tool gives no legal opinion.

3. Editing and redaction

  • “Edit text” places new text over the original and covers the old text. The original text may still exist underneath (it can be copied or extracted). White-out has the same limitation.
  • “Redact” permanently flattens the page into a picture with black boxes burned in, so covered text and images are removed from that page. Always reopen the saved file and confirm the information is really gone before sharing it. Hidden data such as metadata should be removed separately (Document properties → Remove all).
  • Editing a signed document breaks its signature by design.

4. OCR and conversion

Fonts. Text added in Latin-script fonts is embedded as live, selectable text. Indian-language fonts, and any character a chosen font does not contain, are saved as a picture of the text (accurate to look at, but not selectable or searchable).

OCR accuracy depends on scan quality, language and fonts. Conversions to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, HTML and Markdown rebuild content from text positions and may change layout, fonts, tables and images. Review every result.

5. Unsupported features

The browser version does not create password-protected/encrypted PDFs, sign with a certificate or USB token, or design interactive forms.

6. No professional advice

Nothing in the app or on this site is legal, tax, financial or compliance advice. Consult a qualified professional where it matters.
